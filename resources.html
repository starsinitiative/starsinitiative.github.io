Reference Index

External Space Trash Resources

The following organizations provide reliable information, research, data, standards, and policy resources related to orbital debris and the long-term sustainability of space.

A.

Learn About Space Trash

A.01

European Space Agency — Space Debris

ESA Space Debris↗

ESA's main space-debris resource, with information about the orbital environment, debris research, mitigation, collision avoidance, and debris-removal efforts.

A.02

ESA — Space Debris by the Numbers

Space Debris by the Numbers↗

Regularly updated statistics from ESA's Space Debris Office on objects in orbit and the debris environment. This is useful for statistics cited in articles or presentations.

A.03

NASA Orbital Debris Program Office

NASA Orbital Debris Program Office↗

NASA's primary technical resource for orbital-debris research, measurements, modeling, mitigation, and remediation.

A.04

NASA Orbital Debris Quarterly News

Orbital Debris Quarterly News↗

A NASA publication covering recent orbital-debris research, notable debris events, statistics, projects, meetings, and related developments.

B.

Reports and Statistics

B.01

ESA Space Environment Report

ESA Space Environment Report↗

ESA's detailed assessment of the state of Earth's orbital environment and how effectively debris-mitigation measures are being followed. ESA has published the report annually since 2017.

C.

Tracking Satellites and Space Trash

C.01

Space-Track.org

Space-Track.org↗

The U.S. government's public portal for space-situational-awareness information and orbital data. Registration is required for much of its data.

C.02

CelesTrak

CelesTrak Orbital Data↗

A widely used source for current orbital data, including data for active satellites, rocket bodies, debris, and other tracked objects. It also provides documentation useful for students and researchers working with orbital data.

C.03

European Union Space Surveillance and Tracking — EU SST

EU Space Surveillance and Tracking↗

A European space-surveillance organization focused on services including collision-risk assessment, uncontrolled re-entry analysis, and detection of in-orbit fragmentation events.
