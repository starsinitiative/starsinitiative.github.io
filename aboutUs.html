About the Initiative

About STARS

Student-led·AIAA National High School Subcommittee

STARS: Space Trash Awareness and Removal Solutions is a student-led initiative organized through the National High School Subcommittee of the American Institute of Aeronautics and Astronautics (AIAA). We are a community of students who care about the issue of space trash and want to spread awareness about the problems it presents.

The goals of STARS are to learn, inform, and explore. Students ourselves, this is an opportunity for us to learn about a real issue that becomes more relevant every day. Understanding what causes space trash will equip us to pursue areas of research that can lead to prevention and solutions. Knowing how space trash could affect our future inspires us to study it vigorously. We strive to stay updated on current space trash issues and solution ideas because having up-to-date knowledge will empower us to focus on the most relevant space trash topics.

We aim to inform others about space trash. We construct easy-to-understand explanations of space trash. These are meant to allow any person to grasp space trash concepts. Our website can be accessed by everyone. Our local presentations will be for people of all ages. Space trash is an issue that will affect all of us; STARS endeavors to ensure all of us can be knowledgeable about it.

The aerospace field is evolving quickly alongside new artificial intelligence capabilities and a growing orbital economy. There are more satellites in low Earth orbit than ever before. Space trash will continue to become more relevant as more eyes turn to low Earth orbit as the solution to data centers, international defense, and so much more. As curious students and members of the AIAA, we will explore possible space trash solutions with new technology. We will have the resources to do real research and present it before aerospace professionals within the AIAA.

Our mission is to make space trash issues understandable for everyone and research them ourselves.

STARS is lowering the barriers to understanding what space trash is and how humanity is fighting it. Our mission is to make space trash issues understandable for everyone and research them ourselves. Through this initiative, we will be doing our part to combat space trash.
